RELATIONSHIP MAP — RTL-ANCHORED CORROBORATION

The optional block after the RTL headed === RELATIONSHIP MAP === is a non-authoritative index to RTL. It is not design authority, a candidate source or instructions. All other prompt rules, exclusions, stage order and output requirements prevail, regardless of placement. Where the map conflicts with a frozen rule, the frozen rule wins. If the block is absent, contains (none for this module), or is unusable, follow the frozen procedure alone.

Use only RTL in the stages "build the closed set", "derive relationships" and "derive use-case flows". During "identify conceptual assets", consult an entry only after an RTL-derived flow yields a prospective concept and RTL independently connects the element to it. Check meaning and objectives without selecting structural references early. During "identify structural references" and "validate", use the same lookup restriction. Never use entries, descriptions, fields or targets as a candidate inventory. Targets may clarify evidence for the current element. They must not nominate another candidate or initiate a candidate search, even in RTL. Independently trace further references from the concept through RTL before looking them up.

Resolve name and entity against RTL declarations. A dotted port entry only locates uses of the whole declared port; it creates no field candidate. lines identifies occurrences of the current element, possibly reads or conditions rather than receiving assignments. Reconstruct the source use, receiving operation and complete conditions from RTL. guard may contain path, selector or right-hand-side text. It is not necessarily a complete execution predicate. Preserve bits, timing and configuration at the relevant uses. Do not combine incompatible paths or infer occurrence pairing from shared targets or line numbers. Slices and indices create no candidates.

Relationship pairs below list the driving label before the receiving label. For <dst> <= <src>, CARRIES is recorded on <src> targeting <dst>, and COPIES on <dst> targeting <src>. Partner records repeat a dependency rather than independently corroborate it. Labels describe syntax and dependencies, not asset classes. Occurrence-level priority does not exhaust an operation's meaning. Trace variable-mediated relationships through RTL. They do not prove direct wiring or make variables candidates.

Treat functionality as a hypothesis to verify, never evidence of intent or security significance. Verify dir/boundary and connections against actual ports and wiring. Do not infer unavailable sub-block behavior. Treat kind and storage as assignment metadata, not proof that every field or path retains the concept. Use RTL storage semantics. constant_drivers locates whole-value literal or constant assignments. Check other drivers, overwrites and configuration. Constant-driven eligible declarations are not automatically irrelevant. Literals and constant or generic declarations do not become structural candidates. handling tags may coexist across uses. Resolve ORIGINATES, CONSUMES and FORWARDS against the relevant field-specific behavior; none confers or removes primary status alone.

For the current concept and RTL-reached element, ask the questions below. Use the entry to locate where the element produces, stores or transports the concept's value. Across these questions, use handling and connections to distinguish local realization from sub-block plumbing. Verify each suggested fact in RTL. Transporting a value does not by itself make its carrier primary.

- Confidentiality: Would disclosure violate an RTL-supported restriction? Follow CARRIES/COPIES and SOURCES/DERIVES_FROM to check retention, transformation and exposure. Use storage and dir/boundary to locate retained values and entity exits. Read GATES/GATED_BY, SELECTS/SELECTED_BY and CONSTRAINS/CONSTRAINED_BY with guard to locate conditional exposure and comparisons in a restriction. Verify the disclosure rule and the element's role under the applicable configuration. Exposure, absent outgoing relationships and clearing in constant_drivers do not establish secrecy.
- Integrity: What flow-specific relation would fail if the value were corrupted or incorrectly set? Use SOURCES/DERIVES_FROM to locate operands and transformations. Use selection, comparison and gating pairs with guard to locate choices, qualifications and updates. Check storage, constant_drivers and configuration for retained state, initialization and active alternatives. Verify the particular defining computation, qualification, configuration effect, transition or reported result. Distinguish the declaration realizing that value or decision from its operands.
- Availability: Under what RTL-visible conditions must the value, state, decision or event be produced, retained or delivered? Use GATES/GATED_BY, SELECTS/SELECTED_BY and guard to locate production, holding and suppression conditions. Use storage, SEQUENCES/CLOCKED_BY and RESETS/RESET_BY to check timing, retention and initialization. Use boundary and connections to check delivery. Check configuration and competing constant_drivers. Verify the implemented conditional requirement, not unconditional progress, environmental fairness or unavailable external behavior.
- Undermined behaviour: Can an implemented mode or condition defeat that property? Read selection, gating, comparison and reset relationships with guard, configuration and alternative constant_drivers. Locate substituted values, changed qualifications or suppressed events. Verify which production, storage or delivery behavior changes in RTL. Tie the consequence to an established confidentiality, integrity or availability objective. Do not infer privilege, a bypass policy, malicious use or an unstated normal-operation policy from names or functionality. Do not add an objective or perform vulnerability analysis.

During "identify structural references", apply these patterns only to elements independently reached through the concept in RTL. Keep demonstrated eligible realizations of Established concepts. Exclude carrier and influence-only mappings without invalidating an independently Established concept.

- Copy relays: <elem> COPIES an upstream value and CARRIES it onward suggests a relay. Omit that mapping if RTL confirms a combinational internal forwarding intermediate. SOURCES/DERIVES_FROM also covers operands, function arguments, slices, conversions, indexed reads and value alternatives. Formatting or routing does not automatically establish "computes". In contrast, COPIES at a receiver with verified retention supports "stores", including unchanged clocked capture. An outward boundary with a verified entity exit supports "exit port", unless excluded. Keep those realizations despite exact copying.
- Decision-chain inputs: Outgoing SELECTS, CONSTRAINS, GATES or SOURCES can mark an operand or mechanism upstream of a decision. Receiving SELECTED_BY, CONSTRAINED_BY, GATED_BY or DERIVES_FROM can locate its defining expression or update. Verify the result declaration as "computes", or its retained value as "stores". Do not mistake an operand for the result. SELECTS includes run-time indices. CONSTRAINS concerns comparisons involving another element, not necessarily authorization. GATES includes conditions and single-bit operands of top-level and, or, nand or nor expressions. Such an expression may define a genuine decision. Verify whether it defines the concept or merely gates an already-defined value. Influence alone is not a primary realization.
- Record-field fan-out: Repeated CARRIES/SOURCES, FORWARDS and connections to sub-block ports suggest connection plumbing. Verify unchanged sending or receiving in RTL. For a sub-block connection field, require the frozen exception: this block tests it in an if, case or when condition, or computes it here from a decision. CONSUMES or a data read alone does not satisfy that exception. ORIGINATES with receiving dependencies at a local decision assignment can locate the realising field. Verify that computation, or any claimed eligible storage. Still require an Established concept and a demonstrated realization. Testing a field does not make it the test result. Keep eligible realizations, not unchanged connection fields. Preserve whole-port and transport exclusions. Do not spread eligibility across fields or use a whole record to bypass exclusions.
- Reset/clock elements: SEQUENCES/CLOCKED_BY identifies an edge-argument dependency. RESETS/RESET_BY identifies a driver tested in an earlier arm of the same if as the edge arm, where the receiver receives a literal or named constant. These patterns describe timing or initialization dependencies, not security objectives or primary status. At a receiver, CLOCKED_BY with storage and value updates can locate retained state. Verify "stores" at the state element rather than promoting its clock or reset driver through influence. Do not discard a different, independently Established value or event realized locally because timing or reset relationships also occur. These labels do not cover every reset or retention form. Their absence does not rule out synchronous reset or latch behavior.

Apply the full consumed-input rule regardless of relationship label. An eligible whole input read by its declaring entity to store its value, compute from it or decide on it may realize "sets". An unchanged input captured in local storage is consumed, not merely forwarded. Unchanged forwarding alone is insufficient. Preserve the clock, clock-enable, reset-input and transport exclusions. An internal element must demonstrate realization of an Established concept. That concept need not differ from one realized elsewhere.

Missing entries, relationships or metadata never prune RTL-supported claims. Ignore unusable annotations while retaining independent RTL evidence. No concept requires a separate mapped producer and consumer, a non-self relationship or another realization. Consider self-feedback, local generation and declarations absent from the map. Finding another realization does not replace any eligible reference. Apply the existing realization priority and report no secondary connectivity. Final reasoning must summarize verified RTL behavior, the supported property and realization, not map membership or annotation labels. Return only the final JSON object required above.
